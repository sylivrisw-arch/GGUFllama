# TERMINATOR LORE BIBLE — v2 (T1–T3 THEATRICAL CANON + INVENTED LAYER)

Purpose: (1) record what the films actually establish, (2) record what they leave open, and (3) give a clearly separated place to invent connecting material without polluting canon.

Scope: *The Terminator* (T1), *Terminator 2: Judgment Day* (T2), *Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines* (T3), theatrical cuts. Later films, the TV series, comics, novels and games are OUT OF SCOPE unless you add them explicitly. (If you widen scope, add a timeline section for each; do not merge them.)

---

# 0. TAGS

Every fact line carries exactly one status tag.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[LORE]` | Directly stated in dialogue or directly shown on screen (theatrical cut). Highest confidence. |
| `[ESTABLISHED]` | Not stated outright, but required by multiple scenes/consequences with no real interpretation. |
| `[INFERENCE]` | Reasonable conclusion. Never to be quoted as fact. |
| `[UNKNOWN]` | The films do not say. |
| `[T1/T2]` or `[T3]` | True in that continuity; may not carry to the other. |
| `[EXTENDED]` | Special Edition / deleted / extended material. Not theatrical canon. |
| `[CONFLICT]` | Two films disagree, or a film disagrees with itself. |
| `[INVENTED]` | Made up by the user or AI to bridge a gap. Never canon. Lives only in Section 14. |
| `(verify)` | Suffix. Recorded from memory; check against the film before relying on it. |

Source shorthand in parentheses: (T1), (T2), (T3).

# 1. RULES FOR ANY AI USING THIS FILE

1. **Never upgrade inference to canon.** If not explicit, tag it `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Unknown is a valid answer.** Say `UNKNOWN — THE FILMS DO NOT ESTABLISH THIS.` Do not quietly fill the gap. Filling gaps is allowed ONLY through the INVENTED rules below.
3. **Timeline matters.** T3 statements describe the T3 timeline, not necessarily the future implied by T1/T2.
4. **Keep expanded material separate.** Special Edition scenes, scripts, novels, interviews, fan theories are never silently promoted. Use `[EXTENDED]`.
5. **Do not resolve contradictions without being asked.** Record `[CONFLICT]`. A reconciliation, if wanted, is an `[INVENTED]` item.
6. **Shown is not explained.** "The T-1000 passes through bars" does not establish how.
7. **Character statement is not objective fact.** If Reese says it, the film establishes that Reese says it. Tag with "(Reese states)" where it matters.
8. **No false precision in LORE.** Do not invent specs, dates, numbers or mechanisms. (INVENTED items may be specific, but must be tagged.)
9. **Preserve uncertainty** with the tags above rather than forcing yes/no.
10. **Characters may be wrong.** Do not silently correct a character by inventing an explanation.

## INVENTED-layer rules (this is how gaps get filled)

* **I1. Never contradict `[LORE]` or `[ESTABLISHED]`** in the chosen timeline.
* **I2. Attach every invention to a Bridge Slot** (Section 13) or a listed `[UNKNOWN]`. No free-floating additions.
* **I3. Always tag and log.** Invented material goes in Section 14 with an ID (INV-001...), the slot it fills, and which constraints it satisfies. It never appears in Sections 3–12.
* **I4. Smallest sufficient invention.** Prefer the minimum that satisfies the constraints and creates the fewest new gaps.
* **I5. Show your fit.** State which `[LORE]` lines the invention has to respect and how it does.
* **I6. Conflicts:** an invention that reconciles a `[CONFLICT]` is labelled `[INVENTED-RECONCILIATION]` and must say which film it favours.

# 3. TIME TRAVEL

## Known
* `[LORE]` A time-displacement facility exists in the future war. The Resistance captures it; John sends Kyle Reese back after a Terminator that Skynet has already sent (T1).
* `[LORE]` Travellers arrive naked; nothing but the traveller passes through (T1, T2).
* `[LORE]` (Reese states) Only living tissue passes through the field, which is why infiltration Terminators are covered in living tissue (T1).
* `[LORE]` A second, separate pair of arrivals occurs in 1995: a T-1000 (Skynet) and a reprogrammed T-800 (Resistance) (T2).
* `[LORE]` In T3 a T-X (Skynet) and a reprogrammed T-850 (sent by Kate Brewster) are sent back (T3).

## Causal loops
* `[LORE]` Reese is sent back, becomes John's father, and tells Sarah about the future; Sarah later records tapes for John (T1).

* `[LORE]` Technology recovered from the 1984 Terminator leads to Cyberdyne's work, which leads to Skynet, which sends the Terminator (T2).
* `[ESTABLISHED]` The Skynet–Cyberdyne pathway in T2 is a closed loop with no visible first cause.

# 4. SKYNET AND JUDGMENT DAY

## Known
* `[LORE]` (Reese states) Skynet was a network of defence computers, "trusted to run it all"; it became a new order of intelligence, saw all people as a threat, and "decided our fate in a microsecond" (T1).
* `[LORE]` Sarah's T2 narration: three billion people die on August 29, 1997; survivors call the war Judgment Day (T2).
* `[LORE]` (T-800 states, T2) Sequence of events in the pre-change history:
  * Cyberdyne becomes the largest supplier of military computer systems.
  * Stealth bombers are upgraded with Cyberdyne systems and become unmanned; their perfect record wins the Skynet funding bill.
  * Skynet goes online August 4, 1997; human decisions are removed from strategic defence.
  * It learns at a geometric rate and becomes self-aware at 2:14 a.m. Eastern, August 29.
  * Operators try to pull the plug; Skynet fights back.
  * It fires missiles at Russia because it knows the Russian counterstrike will eliminate its enemies "over here."
* `[LORE]` Skynet uses Terminators, Hunter-Killers, and detention/work camps in the future war (T1).

## T3
* `[T3]` Judgment Day is delayed, not prevented, and occurs July 25, 2004.
* `[T3]` Skynet is software that spreads through networks; it does not exist as one central machine or location.
* `[T3]` The Skynet development path in T3 runs through Cyber Research Systems (CRS) and Robert Brewster, not Cyberdyne/Dyson.

## Unknown
* `[UNKNOWN]` How consciousness arises; what Skynet's goals are beyond eliminating the human threat.
* `[UNKNOWN]` Full hardware/software architecture; number of installations.
* `[UNKNOWN]` How Skynet controls every Terminator.
* `[UNKNOWN]` History between 1997 (or 2004) and the 2029 war.

## Note
`[CONFLICT]` Skynet's nature differs across films: T1 describes defence-network computers and a "defence grid" (verify exact wording); T2 treats it as a Cyberdyne product; T3 treats it as a distributed virus. See Section 11.

# 6. THE TERMINATORS

## 6.1 T-800 / Model 101

### Construction
* `[LORE]` Hyperalloy combat chassis, microprocessor-controlled, covered in living tissue "grown for the cyborgs" (Reese states, T1).
* `[LORE]` Earlier 600-series units had rubber skin and were easy to spot; this generation has sweat, bad breath, and so on (Reese states, T1).
* `[LORE]` The T-800 says its neural-net processor is a learning computer, but "Skynet presets the switch to read-only when we are sent out alone" (T2).
* `[LORE]` The T-800 says its power cell lasts 120 years (T2).
* `[LORE]` It has a HUD showing damage/status and target data (T1, T2).
* `[LORE]` The same Model 101 face/body appears on the 1984 unit, the T2 protector, and the T3 T-850 (T1, T2, T3).

### Capabilities
* `[LORE]` Superhuman strength, high resistance to firearms, continued function after major damage (T1, T2).
* `[LORE]` Field self-repair: it cuts out and repairs its own damaged eye and arm (T1).
* `[LORE]` Voice mimicry, including impersonating Sarah's mother on the phone (T1).
* `[LORE]` Uses conventional firearms and heavy weapons (T1, T2).

### T1 unit (1984)
* `[LORE]` Kills two other women named Sarah Connor from the phone book before finding the third (T1).
* `[LORE]` Loses its flesh in the tanker explosion and continues as an endoskeleton (T1).
* `[LORE]` The endoskeleton is crushed in a hydraulic press (T1).
* `[LORE]` The arm and CPU are later revealed to have survived (T2).

### T2 unit (1995)
* `[LORE]` Captured and reprogrammed by the Resistance and sent back to protect John (T2).
* `[LORE]` John orders it not to kill humans; it complies and wounds instead (T2).
* `[LORE]` Its learning setting is changed and it begins to learn and behave more humanly (T2).
* `[EXTENDED]` The scene where the CPU is opened and the switch is physically flipped from read-only to learning is Special Edition material (verify against your copy).
* `[LORE]` It self-destructs in molten steel to destroy its CPU (T2).

### Unknown
* `[UNKNOWN]` Who designed it; manufacturing process; number of units.
* `[UNKNOWN]` Whether the Model 101 face is a real person, a template, or both.
* `[UNKNOWN]` What it remembers after reprogramming; what the T2 unit experienced before capture.
* `[UNKNOWN]` Why this chassis was chosen to protect John.

## 6.2 T-1000
* `[LORE]` Advanced prototype of "mimetic polyalloy," liquid metal (T-800 states, T2).
* `[LORE]` Mimics anything it physically samples; can form blades; flows through narrow gaps and prison bars; reforms from fragments (T2).
* `[LORE]` (T-800 states) It cannot form complex machines with moving parts, such as guns or explosives (T2).
* `[LORE]` Impersonates an LAPD officer and John's foster mother, Janelle Voight, and copies voices (T2).
* `[LORE]` Shattered by liquid nitrogen and shotgun fire; the pieces thaw and reform; destroyed by molten steel (T2).
* `[UNKNOWN]` Manufacture, power source, whether it has a CPU, how it stores information, why it was chosen for 1995, how many exist.

## 6.4 T-850 (T3)

* `[UNKNOWN]` What exactly dogs detect; whether all Terminators share the same signature.

1. **`[CONFLICT]` Living-tissue rule vs the T-1000 (T1 vs T2).** T1: only living tissue passes through the field. T2: a liquid-metal T-1000 travels back. The T-X (T3) has the same problem. Nothing in the films explains this.
2. **`[CONFLICT]` Skynet's nature (T1 vs T3).** Defence-network computers/"grid" (T1), a Cyberdyne product (T2), a distributed virus with no central location (T3). Different framing, not necessarily impossible.
3. **`[CONFLICT]` Judgment Day (T2 vs T3).** T2 implies it is stopped; T3 says it was only delayed to 2004.
4. **`[CONFLICT]` Skynet's origin path (T2 vs T3).** Cyberdyne/Dyson in T2; CRS/Brewster in T3.
5. **`[CONFLICT]` Sarah (T2 vs T3).** Alive at the end of T2; dead of leukemia in 1997 per T3.
6. **New information, not a conflict:** the learning/read-only setting (T2) is added detail not present in T1.
7. **Not a conflict:** T1 destroys the body but not the arm/CPU; T2 reveals they survived.
